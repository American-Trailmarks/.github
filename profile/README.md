# American Trailmarks

### Collect the places that make America unforgettable.

American Trailmarks is a place-collecting experience built around the destinations, routes, landmarks, and stories that make exploring America memorable.

</div>

---

## Explore. Collect. Remember.

American Trailmarks turns real places into a collection worth building. The experience is designed around discovering meaningful destinations, recording where you've been, and building a personal passport of the places that define your travels.

Our visual system draws from classic American park, trail, and travel ephemera while staying distinctly American Trailmarks: **Pine**, **Canyon**, **Sun**, **River**, and **Parchment** form the core palette, anchored by the arched scenic emblem above.

## What we're building

- **Web** — discovery, creation, maps, collections, and the American Trailmarks Passport experience.
- **iOS** — a native companion for collecting and carrying your Trailmarks with you.
- **Trailmarks** — destination-specific collectible artwork that represents individual places while remaining clearly part of the American Trailmarks system.

## Brand principles

**Place first.** The destination and its story should remain the hero.

**Collectible by design.** Trailmarks should feel worth finding, saving, sharing, and remembering.

**American without imitation.** The master brand is original American Trailmarks artwork; destination imagery should respect provenance and never masquerade as an official government seal or protected third-party identity.

**Built for the journey.** The system should work from a 16px favicon to a physical collectible, from a mobile screen to a map full of places.
